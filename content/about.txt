Gopher Ghost
============

Gopher was released at the University of Minnesota in 1991, two years
before the web took off. It was simple: you send one line (a selector),
the server sends back a menu or a document, and the connection closes.

Gopher Ghost brings that protocol back from the dead and uses it to
serve today's internet - Hacker News, RSS feeds, plain text - as clean,
fast, ad-free Gopher menus.

This is a Kiroween 2026 hackathon project (category: Resurrection).
