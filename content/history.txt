A short history of Gopher
-------------------------

1991  The University of Minnesota team releases the Internet Gopher.
1993  RFC 1436 documents the protocol. Gopher traffic is huge.
1993  The university announces licensing fees for its server. Many
      people move to the web, which is free.
1990s Gopher fades, but never fully dies.
2010s+ A small "Gopherspace" community keeps hundreds of servers alive.
