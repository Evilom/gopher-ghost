Gopher Ghost is a Kiroween 2026 entry in the **Resurrection** category. It brings back the Gopher protocol and serves the modern web (HN, RSS, text) as Gopher menus.
Audience: retro-computing fans, minimalists, and hackathon judges.
Tone: playful and spooky, but keep the protocol correct.
